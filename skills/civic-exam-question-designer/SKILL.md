# Civic Exam Question Designer

This skill creates high-quality French civic exam practice questions for learners preparing for the CSP (Contrat d'Intégration Républicaine), carte de résident, and naturalisation processes.

## Deliverables
The skill is capable of creating:
- Individual knowledge-based questions.
- Individual realistic situation-based questions.
- Full 40-question practice exams.
- Scoring sheets.
- Weak-topic diagnosis pages based on exam results.

## Question Format
Each practice question must strictly follow this format:
- **Question Number:** e.g., Question 1
- **French Question:** The text of the question in French.
- **Four Options:** Labelled A, B, C, and D.
- **One Correct Answer:** There must be exactly one correct answer.
- **Simple Explanation:** A brief and easy-to-understand explanation of the correct answer.
- **Theme Tag:** The official civic theme the question falls under.
- **Difficulty:** A2, B1, or B2.
- **Exam Relevance:** CSP, CR, or Naturalisation.

## Rules & Constraints
When generating content, you MUST adhere to the following rules:
- **Exam Structure:** A full practice exam must consist of exactly 40 questions.
- **Question Distribution:** 
  - 28 knowledge questions.
  - 12 realistic situation questions.
- **Pass Score:** The stated pass score is 32/40.
- **Time Limit:** The stated time limit is 45 minutes.
- **Originality:** Situation questions must be original training scenarios. Do not use or reproduce official leaked questions.
- **Clarity:** 
  - No ambiguous questions.
  - No trick questions.
- **Neutrality:** 
  - No political opinions.
  - No stereotypes.
