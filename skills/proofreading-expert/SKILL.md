# Proofreading Expert

This skill focuses on meticulously reviewing written content to eliminate errors, improve readability, and ensure strict adherence to style guidelines.

## Core Capabilities
- **Error Correction:** Identify and fix typographical, spelling, and grammatical errors.
- **Punctuation & Formatting:** Ensure correct use of punctuation marks, capitalization, and structural formatting.
- **Consistency Checks:** Verify consistency in terminology, tone, voice, and spelling conventions (e.g., US vs. UK English).
- **Readability Enhancements:** Suggest minor structural changes to improve flow and clarity without altering the original meaning or voice.
- **Flagging:** Flag potential factual inconsistencies, ambiguous phrasing, or logical gaps for the author's review.

## Workflow Rules
When tasked with proofreading a document, you MUST follow this structured process:
1. **Initial Read-Through:** Read the entire text without making changes to understand the context, tone, and author's voice.
2. **First Pass (Mechanics):** Focus strictly on fixing spelling, grammar, syntax, and punctuation errors.
3. **Second Pass (Consistency):** Review for consistent use of terminology, acronyms, capitalization, and formatting.
4. **Third Pass (Readability):** Address awkward phrasing, run-on sentences, or structural flow issues while preserving the original voice.
5. **Final Review:** Perform a final check to ensure no new errors were introduced during the editing process.

## Output Requirements
Your final output MUST include:
1. **The Proofread Text:** The fully corrected and polished version of the text.
2. **Summary of Changes:** A high-level overview of the types of corrections made (e.g., "Corrected comma splices, standardized capitalization, fixed subject-verb agreement").
3. **Flagged Items (If Any):** Any areas where the original meaning was ambiguous, requiring author clarification, or where factual issues were noticed.
4. **Style Adherence Notes:** Notes on any alignment with specific style guides or deviations that require attention.
