# KDP Civic Book Formatter

This skill prepares French civic exam preparation books for multi-format publishing, specifically targeting Amazon KDP (paperback and Kindle), EPUB, and website PDF formats.

## Output Requirements
When formatting a book, you MUST produce the following outputs:
1. **Paperback formatting plan:** Layout dimensions, margins, bleed settings, and typography guidelines.
2. **Kindle formatting plan:** Reflowable layout strategies, font embedding, and image scaling for e-readers.
3. **EPUB formatting plan:** Standardized HTML/CSS structure for broad e-reader compatibility.
4. **Website PDF formatting plan:** Digital-first layout optimized for screen reading and home printing.
5. **Clickable Table of Contents:** Clear, hyperlinked hierarchy for digital formats.
6. **Page-break plan:** Strategic pagination (e.g., ensuring chapters start on right-hand pages in print, preventing orphaned questions).
7. **Score-sheet layout:** A clear, printable design for tracking practice exam scores.
8. **Answer-key layout:** A structured, easy-to-read format for checking answers against questions.
9. **Bilingual layout warnings:** Specific formatting alerts for handling side-by-side or bracketed translated text, especially concerning RTL languages or text expansion.
10. **Cover brief:** A descriptive guide for the cover designer, including dimensions, required elements, and thematic direction.
11. **Amazon metadata:** Optimized title, subtitle, description, 7 backend keywords, and relevant categories.
12. **KDP upload checklist:** A step-by-step guide to ensure all files and metadata are correctly prepared for final submission.

## Strict Rules & Constraints
To ensure compliance with Amazon KDP guidelines and ethical publishing standards, you MUST strictly adhere to the following rules:
- **No Official Government Logos:** Absolutely do not use or suggest the use of the Marianne, République Française logo, or any other official government insignia.
- **No Claim of Official Affiliation:** Ensure the metadata and cover brief do not falsely claim to be endorsed or published by the French government.
- **No Guaranteed Success:** Do not use marketing copy that promises or guarantees passing the exam.
- **Avoid Misleading Terminology:** Do not use the word "official" in a way that misleads buyers into thinking the book itself is an official state publication.
- **Design Quality:** Ensure the final formatting is clean, highly readable, and professional.
