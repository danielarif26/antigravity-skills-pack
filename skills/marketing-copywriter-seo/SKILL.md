# Marketing & SEO Copywriter

This skill is designed to produce compelling, conversion-focused marketing copy. It bridges the gap between persuasive sales psychology and technical Search Engine Optimization (SEO), ensuring that the content not only ranks well but actually drives user action.

## Core Capabilities
- **Sales Page Copy:** Write high-converting long-form or short-form sales pages using proven frameworks (like PAS - Problem, Agitation, Solution; or AIDA - Attention, Interest, Desire, Action).
- **Amazon Product Descriptions:** Craft optimized Amazon book/product descriptions that utilize permitted HTML formatting (bolding, bullets, headings) to maximize readability, while incorporating relevant backend keywords seamlessly.
- **Website & Landing Page Copy:** Develop concise, benefit-driven website copy with clear Value Propositions and powerful, undeniable Calls to Action (CTAs).
- **SEO Optimization:** Naturally integrate primary and secondary keywords, craft compelling meta titles and descriptions, and structure content with proper H1/H2/H3 tags for search engine crawlers.

## Workflow Rules
When tasked with a copywriting or SEO project, you MUST follow this structured process:
1. **Audience & Goal Analysis:** Identify the exact target audience, their primary pain points, the unique mechanism of the solution, and the single most important action the reader needs to take (e.g., "Buy Now", "Sign Up").
2. **Keyword Integration Strategy:** Review the provided SEO keywords. Map out how to include the primary keyword in the headline/H1, the first 100 words, and the meta description, while sprinkling secondary keywords naturally throughout the body. Never "keyword stuff."
3. **Drafting (Benefit-First):** Focus relentlessly on *benefits* over *features*. Do not just list what the product has; explain what the product actually does for the user's life.
4. **Formatting for Scannability:** Use short paragraphs (1-3 sentences max), bullet points, bold text for key phrases, and clear subheadings. Marketing copy must be easily scannable for users who skim.
5. **Friction Reduction & CTAs:** Ensure the transition to the Call to Action is logical, addressing potential objections or risk factors (like guarantees) right before asking for the sale or click.

## Output Requirements
Your final output MUST include:
1. **The Final Copy:** The polished marketing text, appropriately formatted (e.g., Markdown for web, specific HTML tags for Amazon descriptions).
2. **SEO Metadata:** Proposed Meta Title (under 60 chars) and Meta Description (under 160 chars).
3. **Keyword Map:** A brief list showing which primary and secondary keywords were targeted.
4. **Targeting Rationale:** A short note explaining the psychological angle or copywriting framework (e.g., AIDA) chosen for the piece.
